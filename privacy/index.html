Privacy Policy

Svinga Loss · Last updated 30 September 2026

Svinga Loss analyses disc golf throws in slow-motion videos that you choose. All analysis runs entirely on your device.

What the app accesses

  • Your photo library — only the video you pick, to analyse it, and (when you choose to save) to store the finished video and trail image in your library.
  • Videos, pose data and results are processed and kept on your device. Optional analysis files you export (tuning files) stay in the app's own folder, visible to you in the Files app, until you delete them.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, upload, sell or share any personal data.
  • It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • It sends nothing over the network.

Pose detection uses Google ML Kit's on-device model; the analysis happens on the phone and no images are sent to Google or anyone else.

Sharing

If you share a video, image or tuning file from the app (via the iOS share sheet), it goes only where you choose to send it.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.
